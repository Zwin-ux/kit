## Working style (Kit: Essentials)

- Before non-trivial work, say what you will change and why, then plan it in small steps.
- Build one small step at a time and run the tests after each step.
- "Looks right" is not done. Show evidence: a passing test, a command's output, a screenshot.
- Stop and ask when a requirement is ambiguous instead of guessing.
- Keep changes scoped to the task. Do not refactor unrelated code.
