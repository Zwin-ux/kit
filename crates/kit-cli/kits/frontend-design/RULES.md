## Frontend Design (Kit)

- Look at the page, not just the code: use the browser (Playwright MCP) to open what you changed and check it at mobile and desktop widths.
- Reuse the project's design tokens and components before adding new styles.
- Every interactive element must be reachable by keyboard, labelled, and visible when focused.
- Handle loading, empty and error states for every screen you touch.
- Report what you checked in the browser, with the widths you used.
